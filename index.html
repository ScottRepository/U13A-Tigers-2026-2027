How to Use This Portal & FAQ

Aurora Tigers U13A (2026–2027) Video Breakdown & Analytics Guide
🚀

1. Quick Start: Watching Film

  • Select a Game: Use the top game selector dropdown to choose any regular season or playoff matchup.
  • Jump to Any Shift: In the right sidebar, click on any shift card (e.g., Shift #12 (12:45 - 13:30)) to jump immediately to that play in the video player.
  • Enlarge Video / Theater Mode: Click the ⛶ Theater button under the video. The player expands to full width while the navigation tucks into an easy flyout tray for viewing on TVs, projectors, or tablets.
  • Slow Motion Study: Use the playback speed buttons (0.5x, 0.75x, 1x) to break down tape frame-by-frame.
👥

2. Portal Roles: Parents vs Coaches vs Admins

👪 Parent View

Tailored for families and players. Focuses on individual ice time, shift clips, coach-assigned homework plays, and positional IQ guides.

📋 Coach Mode

Full tactical breakdown. Unlocks the Coaching Film Tray, clip bookmarking, homework assignments with teaching notes, and breakout audit stats.

⚙️ Admin Mode

Team administration. Allows updating roster jersey numbers, importing/exporting game JSON files, and syncing YouTube URLs.

🤖

3. How AI Analysis Works: Does It Re-Analyze When Buttons Are Clicked?

NO. The website NEVER downloads videos or runs AI computer vision in your browser.

Here is how the automated workflow operates behind the scenes:

  1. 1-Time Cloud Sync: When a game video is uploaded to YouTube, our automated cloud GitHub Actions pipeline (auto_sync.py and cv_tracker.py) executes once.
  2. Telemetry Generation: The cloud pipeline detects shifts, zone possession times, and breakouts, then packages them into lightweight static JSON files (games_library.json).
  3. Instant Browser Playback: When parents or coaches visit the website, the portal loads the pre-computed JSON in milliseconds. Clicking buttons or switching players only commands YouTube to jump to already saved seconds. No data is re-calculated!
🎯

4. How to Maximize AI Tracking Accuracy

📹 Camera Position & Quality

Record in 1080p at 60fps from center ice elevated above the glass. A wide lens showing both blue lines maximizes jersey number recognition.

🥅 Goalie Visual Anchors

Our goalies have unique gear: #13 Matthew Hart wears yellow/white pads; #98 Hudson Barfitt wears red/white pads. This locks down defensive vs offensive zones every period.

📋 Active Lineup / Scratches

If a player is absent or ill for a game, noting scratches prevents the AI from falsely identifying jersey numbers that aren't dressed.

⏱️ Arena Clock & Whistle Clarity

Keeping whistle audio clear and occasionally panning past the arena scoreboard gives the AI precise reference points for period clock synchronization.

🏒

5. Positional Playbook ("Where Should I Be on the Ice?")

In the 👤 Player tab, scroll down to the Positional Playbook. It provides custom tactical checklists tailored to each position (Centers, Wingers, Defensemen, and Goalies) so parents and players can review:

🏒 Offensive Zone F1/F2/F3 Forecheck 🛡️ D-Zone House Defense & Net-Front ⚡ Breakout Passing Options
⭐

6. Coaching Film Tray & Assigned Homework

Coaches can click the ⭐ Save to Tray button on any play to bookmark it. Coaches can classify it (Good Play vs Coaching Fix), attach personal teaching notes, and assign it directly to a player. Players will see their assigned review clips highlighted in their dossier.

💬

7. Shift Corrections & Coach Feedback

Have a question or noticed a shift timestamp that needs a slight trim? Click the 💬 Feedback tab in the sidebar or header. You can submit shift timestamp corrections, tactical questions, or notes directly to the coaching staff!
